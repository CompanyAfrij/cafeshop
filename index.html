Launch Roadmap

From idea to open doors

A realistic step-by-step path to opening. You don't need everything perfect — you need a clean, honest start and the discipline to improve weekly.

Month 1 — Foundation
Plan, Permit, and Secure Location
Finalize the shop space near Al-Hilal Central College. Apply for local business registration and food handler permits. Create a simple budget. Decide on shop name and basic look.
Month 2 — Setup
Fit-Out & Equipment
Buy essential equipment: tea/coffee boiler, fridge, seating, display shelf for snacks. Keep decor minimal but warm — wooden tones, good lighting, a clean counter. You don't need fancy, you need clean and welcoming.
Month 2–3 — Prep
Supplier Relationships & Test Menu
Source tea, milk, and snack ingredients locally. Do a soft test run with 10 trusted people. Refine the top 3 drinks before you open publicly. Price everything fairly — your reputation is more valuable than margin in month one.
Month 3 — Launch
Grand Opening
Open near the start of a school term for maximum foot traffic. Offer a launch special (free tea with snack purchase) for the first week. Hand out small flyers near the school gate. Let the quality do the rest.
Month 4–6 — Grow
Build Regulars & Expand Offerings
Track your top sellers and cut what doesn't move. Build loyalty — know your regulars' names and orders. Add 2–3 new items based on demand. Consider a simple WhatsApp status to post daily specials.
Marketing & Growth

How to grow in a small town

In a place like Thambala, word-of-mouth is worth more than any ad. Your strategy should focus on relationships, consistency, and being genuinely good.

📣 Marketing Tactics
  • WhatsApp status — post daily specials & new items
  • Facebook Page — local community visibility
  • School gate flyers on exam and event days
  • Student loyalty card — every 5th tea is free
  • Partner with school canteen for referrals
  • Seasonal specials — Ramadan drinks, New Year treats
🎯 Customer Segments
  • School students (daily regulars, volume)
  • Teachers & school staff (premium buyers)
  • Local families (weekend sit-down visits)
  • Commuters passing through town
  • Small business owners in the area
⭐ What Makes You Different
  • Only café in Thambala — you're the category
  • School-adjacent = convenience no one else offers
  • Friendly, personalized service
  • Fair local pricing — not "city prices"
  • Clean, calm space to sit — rare in small towns
⚠️ Watch Out For
  • Inconsistent quality — your regulars will notice
  • Overspending on decor before revenue is proven
  • Menu too wide too soon — stick to basics first
  • No record-keeping — track daily sales from day 1
  • Ignoring school holidays in your cash flow plan
